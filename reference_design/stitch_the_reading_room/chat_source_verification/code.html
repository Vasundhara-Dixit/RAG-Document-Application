What are our rollover policies for accrued PTO when transitioning between subsidiary entities, and who needs to approve exception waivers?

Answer Archival Synthesis
verified_user Citation Alignment: Complete

Accrued PTO transitions between legal subsidiaries operate under an automatic rollover baseline, restricted to a standard balance threshold of eighty total hours upon executing bilateral transfer paperwork . Any accrued leave accrued beyond this 80-hour statutory ceiling does not forfeit immediately but instead converts into deferred cash-settlement credits calculated according to the transferor company’s baseline scale, unless formal harmonization terms are established prior to Day 1 of reporting .

If an employee requests an exception to carry over their complete accumulated PTO balance exceeding 80 hours directly into the successor entity, this demands concurrent bilateral authorization. Specifically, the waiver must receive formal counter-signatures from both the Division Vice President of People and the presiding Group Controller at least fourteen calendar days prior to the effective transfer date . Submissions without both counter-signatures default to mandatory liquidation of excess hours.
